4. OpenAlex fields we actually request

Works select (collect.py L87–88): id, doi, title, publication_year, type, primary_location, cited_by_count, authorships, referenced_works, primary_topic

Authors select (L109): id, display_name, summary_stats, works_count, cited_by_count → we keep h from summary_stats.h_index

Filter: affiliation in our 4 institute IDs, dates 2016-01-01 … 2026-12-31, types article | review | proceedings-article (collect.py L89–90).

5. What is computed by us (not downloaded as a ready-made score)

  • Faculty flag — threshold ≥5 papers in this corpus (build.py L18). OpenAlex has no faculty field.
  • Q1 share — Q1 count / (Q1+Q2+Q3+Q4), Unknown excluded (build.py L203–204).
  • IF bands — ISSN lookup in journal_if.py; Unlisted if missing.
  • Internal citation graph / PageRank / weighted PageRank — NetworkX on our papers only (build.py L230–253).
  • Louvain communities, modularity, betweenness, degree — NetworkX (build.py L277–300). Colours on the authorship graph = these communities, not institutes.
  • In/out Indian citation flow and Jaccard — our counters (build.py L307–358).
  • Dashboard drawing — only ~250 most-connected nodes (build.py keep[:250] in cit_stats / auth_stats). Full counts are in the note under the graph.
